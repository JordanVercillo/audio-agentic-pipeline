What this is, and what it isn't

This is one person's library, not a survey of recorded music. The 1970s row is “the 1970s tracks in this corpus”, which is a much narrower thing than “1970s music”. The direction of both trends matches the published literature; the magnitudes are this corpus's own.

Two more caveats stated rather than silently corrected: release dates are Spotify's, so a remaster reads as its reissue year — some older records are counted in a later decade. And loudness is measured from the audio we acquired, so a loud transfer of a quiet master reads loud. Decades with fewer than {{ min_tracks }} tracks are dropped entirely rather than drawn from a handful of songs.
