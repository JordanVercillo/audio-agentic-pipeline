How to read these numbers
Corpus
Every song we've analyzed — the shared yardstick your taste is compared against.
Standard deviation (σ)
How spread out the corpus is on a feature. One σ ≈ one typical step from the average.
A σ chip, e.g. +0.2σ
Your average sits 0.2 of a typical step above the corpus (a minus sign = below). Bigger = more distinctive; past about ±2σ you're in the top or bottom few percent.
σ-shift (taste drift)
The same σ ruler applied over time: how far your recent sound moved from your all-time average. Near 0 is steady.
Percentile
“more mainstream than 63%” means 63 of every 100 corpus songs rank below your average — a rank from 0–100, not a score.
