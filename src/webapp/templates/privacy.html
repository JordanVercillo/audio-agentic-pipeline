Privacy

This is a personal analytics pilot. The design principle is simple: we analyze songs, not people.

What we access

When you log in with Spotify, this app requests read-only access to {% for s in scopes %}{{ s }}{% if not loop.last %}{% if loop.revindex0 == 1 %} and {% else %}, {% endif %}{% endif %}{% endfor %} — your top tracks and artists, and (so you can import a playlist into your library) the list of your own and collaborative playlists and their tracks. We only read playlist contents when you click Analyze on a specific playlist. Authorization uses Spotify's PKCE flow against a public client id; no app secret exists anywhere in this system.

What we keep — and what we don't

Your listening data is ephemeral. Your top tracks and artists live only in a server-side session that expires automatically ({{ ttl_minutes }} minutes of inactivity) or when you log out. They are never written to a database, never shared, never sold.

What we cache is song-keyed, not person-keyed: the acoustic features of tracks (tempo, energy, timbre — derived by our own signal processing) and a spectrogram image per track. Those describe the song and contain nothing about who listened to it.

Audio is never stored or served. When a song is analyzed, the audio is fetched transiently, processed, and deleted; only derived features and the spectrogram image are kept.

AI answers & quality logging

The AI features (“ask your taste”, the taste profile, and the chat) run on a local model on the developer's own machine — your data is not sent to any third-party AI service. The model receives only the facts already shown on your dashboard (your session's tracks, clusters, drift) to write the reply; without a model configured, answers are generated locally from a template.

We log AI conversations for quality review. To measure and improve accuracy, each AI question and its answer are recorded — the question, the data context the model was given, and the reply — tagged with a random conversation id, not your account. These logs are kept for up to 90 days and used only to review answer quality; they are never shared or sold.

Contact
