Privacy

This is a personal analytics pilot. The design principle is simple: we analyze songs, not people.

What we access

When you log in with Spotify, this app requests one scope — user-top-read — your top tracks and artists. Authorization uses Spotify's PKCE flow against a public client id; no app secret exists anywhere in this system.

What we keep — and what we don't

Your listening data is ephemeral. Your top tracks and artists live only in a server-side session that expires automatically ({{ ttl_minutes }} minutes of inactivity) or when you log out. They are never written to a database, never shared, never sold.

What we cache is song-keyed, not person-keyed: the acoustic features of tracks (tempo, energy, timbre — derived by our own signal processing) and a spectrogram image per track. Those describe the song and contain nothing about who listened to it.

Audio is never stored or served. When a song is analyzed, the audio is fetched transiently, processed, and deleted; only derived features and the spectrogram image are kept.

Optional AI answers

If the “ask your taste” answers are LLM-written, the model receives only the facts already shown on your dashboard (your session's tracks, clusters, drift) to write the reply — nothing is retained by this app beyond your session. Without an LLM configured, answers are template-generated locally.

Contact

Questions or want anything removed? Email vercillojordan@gmail.com. Removal is trivial by design: log out (or let the session expire) and nothing about you remains.
